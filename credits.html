The Crownless King

A text-driven branching narrative spanning three acts, a cast of five, and more ways to lose the crown than to keep it.

Created By

Story, Writing Design — M. Chow

Programming — M. Chow

Built With

HTML5, CSS3 — Structure and visual design, including the theming system and responsive layout.

Vanilla JavaScript — All branching logic, the turn-based throne-room battle, party selection, and the Maven betrayal system are written in plain JavaScript with no external libraries.

Typography — Palatino Linotype / Georgia (system serif stack).

Images

Scene illustrations and character portraits sourced from Pinterest boards and generated reference imagery. All images are used for personal, non-commercial storytelling purposes.

Acknowledgements

Inspired by stories and games like Telltale's The Walking Dead, Life is Strange, Until Dawn and other titles. The branching narrative structure is influenced by the works of authors like George R.R. Martin and video game writers like Chris Avellone.

Special thanks to anyone who played through to an ending they did not expect.
