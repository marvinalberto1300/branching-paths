About the Story

The Crownless King

The Crownless King is a text-driven branching narrative. You play a ruler stripped of their crown, fighting through betrayal, exile, and open war to reclaim a throne that was never truly safe to begin with.

Every scene presents a decision. Those decisions accumulate — shaping who fights alongside you, how well-armed your forces are, and which endings remain within reach when the final confrontation arrives.

How Choices Work

Safe choices are cautious, measured moves. They tend to preserve resources and companions at the cost of momentum.

Risky choices are bold gambles. The payoff can be significant, but failure often costs lives or closes paths permanently.

Emotional choices are driven by loyalty, conscience, or desperation. They can open unique story branches unavailable through pure strategy.

There is no single correct path. Every route through the story is valid, and some of the most interesting outcomes come from unexpected combinations of choices.

The Cast

The King — The protagonist. Crownless, but not powerless. Every decision flows through them.

Brann — A cleric whose calm voice and healing hands have kept more than one lost cause alive. Loyal by nature, though loyalty has its limits.

Elya — An archer of few words and precise aim. She follows the King not from duty but from a conviction she rarely explains.

Harold — A knight who believes in order above all. He is most useful when someone tells him clearly what order to defend.

Maven — A mage whose knowledge runs deeper than comfort allows. Her allegiance is conditional, and the condition is not always obvious until it is too late.

Act Structure

Act I — The flight. The capital has fallen and the King is exposed. Early choices determine which allies survive the escape and what resources carry into the campaign.

Act II — The gathering. Scattered across marsh and mountain, potential allies must be recruited, persuaded, or abandoned. The route taken here determines who stands with you in Act III.

Act III — The assault. Armed with whoever remains, the King marches on the throne. A turn-based confrontation plays out in the throne room, shaped by everything that came before.
