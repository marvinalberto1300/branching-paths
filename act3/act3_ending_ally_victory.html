Ending III

Ally's Victory

You hold the line. That is no small thing. While the Usurper's guard pressed against you, blade for blade, someone else found the opening you could not — and took it.

The tyrant falls. The hall goes quiet.

You are standing. That surprises you more than it should. You look to your companion across the throne room and they look back, chest heaving, their weapon still in their hand. Neither of you speaks for a long moment.

The crown is returned. The kingdom breathes again. In the days that follow, the songs will name you both — the one who held, and the one who struck. You are not sure how you feel about that. You are not sure you are supposed to feel one way about it.

What you know is this: without you, they never would have reached him. Without them, you would have died holding a line that led nowhere. Victory was never going to belong to one pair of hands.

The throne stands. You are alive. Some battles are won together, or not at all.
