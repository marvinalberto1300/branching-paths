Neutral Ending

A Costly Peace

The words are enough. The Usurper stands down. His soldiers lower their weapons, and the fighting stops.

You have done what seemed impossible — you talked your way back from the edge. But the cost of those words is written in the faces of those who are no longer here to hear them.

The throne room smells of blood and tallow. The empty spaces where your companions once stood feel heavier than stone.

The Usurper is brought to trial, as he should be. The tribunal is fair, the verdict just. The kingdom is given a chance to heal — and it will, in time.

But you will carry this with you. Every time you speak of the peace you won, there will be a name — or more than one — that you do not say aloud. People who believed in you enough to follow you here and never made it out.

Peace was the right outcome. You still wonder if you could have found it sooner. You still wonder if the cost was yours to ask.

It is a victory. It does not feel like one.
